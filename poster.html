Gulf Escalation
Iran strikes U.S. bases in the Gulf
A day after U.S. jets hit an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran fired drones and missiles at U.S. installations at Ali Al Salem air base in Kuwait and in Bahrain on Wednesday. No casualties were reported.
Europe & War
Kerch Bridge road span collapses into the sea
Ukraine's General Staff confirmed Wednesday that two nights of drone and missile strikes brought down the 227-metre road arch of the Crimea bridge. Two people were killed; Russia said it shot down a record 1,441 drones in 24 hours.
Asia–Pacific
Xi greets Trump on the tarmac as APEC opens
Xi Jinping welcomed Donald Trump at Bao'an airport, the first tarmac greeting of his presidency, as the APEC leaders' meeting opened in Shenzhen. Xi and Putin met at midday; a first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral is set for Thursday.
Health — Africa
Ebola in Congo more than doubles: 11,000 cases, 5,400 dead
The WHO said the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak more than doubled in three months to over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths across 67 health zones, and on Wednesday kept it a global health emergency.
Health — Americas
U.S. loses measles-free status after 26 years
The Pan American Health Organization stripped the U.S. of measles elimination status, held since 2000, after the same strain circulated for over a year. The CDC counted 5,412 cases in 2026, the most since 1991.
United States
Peltola flips Alaska Senate seat; Democrats take 56–44
Democrat Mary Peltola beat Republican Dan Sullivan with 50.1% of the vote, becoming the first Alaska Native elected to the U.S. Senate. The result, called Tuesday night, gives Democrats 56 of 100 seats.
