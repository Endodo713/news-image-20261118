War

Iran strikes U.S. bases in the Gulf

A day after U.S. jets struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran fired drones and missiles at U.S. installations at Ali Al Salem air base in Kuwait and in Bahrain on Wednesday. Bahrain said it was its third interception that week. No casualties were reported.
Ukraine

Kerch Bridge road span collapses into the sea

Ukraine's General Staff confirmed Wednesday that two nights of drone and missile strikes brought down the 227-metre road arch of the Crimea bridge.
Diplomacy

Xi breaks precedent to greet Trump on the tarmac

Xi Jinping personally met Donald Trump on the tarmac at Shenzhen airport — a hug at the foot of Air Force One — as the APEC summit opened.
Health

Ebola in Congo more than doubles: 11,000 cases

The U.N. said the Ebola outbreak had more than doubled in three months to over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths.
Health

U.S. loses measles-free status after 26 years

The Pan American Health Organization stripped the U.S. of measles elimination status, held since 2000. More than 5,400 cases and 10 deaths were reported in 2026.
Politics

Peltola flips Alaska Senate seat; Democrats take 56–44

Democrat Mary Peltola beat Republican Dan Sullivan with 50.1% of the vote, becoming the first Alaska Native elected to the U.S. Senate. The result, called Tuesday night, gives Democrats 56 of 100 seats.
